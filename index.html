Independent Research

Information, Emergent Geometry & Mathematical Foundations

An independent research program at the intersection of information dynamics, emergent spacetime, additive combinatorics, complex systems, and information economics.

This site records formal preprints, computational experiments, auditable candidate results, and reproducible research code. Exploratory hypotheses are explicitly separated from verified mathematical results.

Research Program

Four connected research directions

The program explores how information structure, geometry, computation, and collective dynamics may generate higher-level mathematical and physical behavior.

01

Structural Information Evolution

Information dynamics, structural constraints, emergence, and the evolution of complex systems.

02

Emergent Geometry & Causality

Investigating whether geometric and causal structures can arise from deeper informational organization.

03

Additive Combinatorics & Computation

Computational audits, additive structure, covering problems, and candidate counterexamples.

04

Information Economics

Information-theoretic approaches to market dynamics, phase transitions, and collective economic behavior.
