Research Programme

Four connected research directions

The programme explores how information structure, geometry, computation, and collective dynamics may generate higher-level mathematical and physical behaviour.

01

Structural Information Evolution

Information dynamics, structural constraints, emergence, and the evolution of complex systems across scales.

02

Emergent Geometry & Causality

Causal structure, Lorentzian geometry, light-cone algebra, quotient constructions, and pre-geometric descriptions of spacetime.

03

Spectral & Geometric Dynamics

Spectral dimension, scale-dependent geometry, irreversible flow, fractal structures, and related geometric dynamics.

04

Mathematical Foundations & Computation

Representation problems, additive combinatorics, gap geometry, computational enumeration, and reproducible analysis.
