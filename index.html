Sun's (2,4,6,8) Conjecture
Computational Counterexample Candidate
n* = 896,315,812,331,399
OEIS: A306477 Claim level: Unconfirmed computational candidate
\[ n = \binom{w}{2} + \binom{x}{4} + \binom{y}{6} + \binom{z}{8} \]

The V23.4 phase completed an exhaustive computational investigation of the candidate \(n^* = 896{,}315{,}812{,}331{,}399\) within the mathematically bounded search domain. The computation examined 2,818,953,028 admissible \((z,y,x)\) triples using exact integer arithmetic and found no representation of the candidate.

The result is classified as a computational counterexample candidate, not a confirmed mathematical counterexample. Independent replication and further mathematical audit remain necessary before a stronger conclusion can be drawn.

The V23.4 investigation also established the modular result \(\mathcal{D}_3 \pmod{231} = \mathbb{Z}/231\mathbb{Z}\), ruling out a local residue obstruction at that modulus.
